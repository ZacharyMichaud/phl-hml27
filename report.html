How the grades work

Source. The official league saves' trade histories: every GM-to-GM trade since the league opened in September 2026. AI trades in the game's other leagues are left out, and so are waiver claims and offer sheets, which the league enters in the game as trades. A season runs from July 1 to June 30, the league year; pick one at the top of the page to grade it alone.

Value. The game's own trade valuation of each side at the time of the deal, as the save records it. Value in is what a team received; value out is what it sent. A trade is a Win or a Loss for a team only when the gap is more than of the value moved; inside that band it is Even, a deal both sides' AI GMs would accept. Even trades count as neither a win nor a loss, but their value still counts toward Edge.

Edge. (value in − value out) ÷ (value in + value out): value won as a share of value moved, so a busy GM and a quiet one compare fairly. The grade comes from Edge using the cutoffs below. A team needs at least trades for a grade (fewer shows INC) and for a full grade; in between, the grade carries an asterisk.

Combined trades. The game allows at most 5 assets per side in one trade, so bigger deals get entered as several trades on the same day between the same two teams. Those are combined into one deal here and graded as a whole; their trade number joins the parts, like 3844+3845+3846.

Trade cards. Click any trade to see every asset: each player's overall and potential at the trade and today, and each asset's value at the trade and today. At the trade, each side's recorded value is split across its assets by their values in the save of the trade day; where those don't add up to it, the gap is spread over the side's players. Today is the save of (or the last save that holds a value). A player who has since left as a free agent or retired counts as 0, and a used pick counts as the player drafted with it.

What it measures. Asset value, not whether a trade helped a team win. Cap dumps, rentals and win-now veterans for young talent count as value lost. Where a team took picks to retain salary, that shows as value won, since the game does not price the cap cost. In a three-team trade, what each team sent is split between the other two by each asset's value on the trade day. Players are shown with their overall and age as of the latest save, not at trade time.
