How to read it

Cap hit. Every contract on a team's NHL roster, plus dead cap: salary it kept on a player it traded, and buyouts. Contracts in the minors, junior or Europe don't count, and neither do unsigned draft picks. Space is the cap less that total; a negative number is a team over the cap.

By the day. The game charges the cap one day at a time over the -day season. "Charged so far" is the game's own running total for the team; "On pace for" adds today's roster for every day that is left.

Later seasons. What today's NHL roster and dead cap already commit, with signed extensions counted from the season they start. They are set against today's cap, since next season's cap isn't known yet.

Free agents. A player is unrestricted at or after seven NHL seasons, restricted otherwise. That is worked out here from the league's free-agency settings, not read from the game, so treat a borderline case with care.

Clauses. NTC is a no-trade list; hover it for the teams. Clause is a no-move or a full no-trade clause.
