How it works

Monthly ticks. On the 2nd of each month from November to March the game moves ratings, usually by a point or two, and tells each GM about his own players. Those reports stay in the league save, so this page lists every team's. A player needs room under his ceiling and games played to move; almost nobody 29 or older does.

Summer. The big step. Over the summer everyone ages a year: prospects can jump several points, and some players 34 and older lose a point or two. There is no league-wide report for it, so the summer rows compare each player's rating in the last save before the rollover with the first one after.

Club. Where the player was when his rating moved: the NHL club, or the league he was playing in. For the summer it is the league he played most of the season before in. Potential is his badge at that moment, in the game's colours.
