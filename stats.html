How it works

The numbers. Each player's season line as the league save holds it: the same totals the game's own stats screens show, and the NHL lines add up to every team's goals, shots and record. A traded player gets one line for the season, with every team he played for. Age is today's for this season, and on February 1 for an earlier one.

Rates. Per 60 uses all of his ice time, at any strength. P/ is his points per game over games. A1 is primary assists, and P1 is goals plus primary assists. Rates and Value open at a minimum number of games so that one good night doesn't top the list; change it with Minimum.

On ice. GF and GA are the goals scored while he was on the ice, power-play goals left out; +/− is the difference and GF% his team's share. CF and CA are shot attempts at even strength while he was on the ice, CF% his team's share.

Physical. The game gives every penalty two minutes, so PIM is twice his penalties. Pen ± is penalties drawn less penalties taken. FO% shows for players who take at least four faceoffs a game.

Value. Cap hit is this season's, and OVR is today's rating. $/point is the cap hit divided by his points over games: what a point costs at his scoring pace. It says nothing about his defence.

Goalies. L is losses in regulation. GSAA is goals saved above average: the goals the league's average goalie would have allowed on the same shots, less the goals he allowed. The save percentage leader needs a third of the busiest goalie's games.

Rookies. R marks a player in his first NHL season, as the game counts it.

Other leagues. Players whose NHL rights a club holds today, in the league they are playing in. Team is that NHL club. GSAA there is against every goalie in that league.
